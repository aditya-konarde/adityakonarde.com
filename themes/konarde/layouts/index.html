Questions

Before you subscribe.

When does the newsletter come out?
Every Friday. The first issue went out in {{ $first.Date.Format "January 2006" }}, and there have been {{ len $issues }} so far.
How do I subscribe?
With any RSS reader. Use the newsletter feed for the weekly issue only, or the full feed for everything. There is no email list.
Is the newsletter written by AI?
AI helps gather the source material. I read every story before an issue goes out, and every story links to where it came from.
Do you track readers?
No. There are no analytics, no cookies and no third party scripts. The privacy page has the details.
Can I invite you to speak?
Yes. I speak at meetups and conferences about cloud native systems and engineering practice. Send me a message on LinkedIn.
What do you work with every day?
Grafana, Prometheus, Loki, Tempo and a fair number of AI agents. The setup page lists the hardware and tools.
